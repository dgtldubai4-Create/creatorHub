About Khushii

Some photographers wait for the light. In a live room the light never waits — so I learned to read it, and to move first.

I'm a Dubai-based photographer working across live performance and artist stories. Six years in the pit have taught me where the confetti falls, when a crowd is about to erupt, and how to work a stage wash without killing the mood the lighting designer built. I've shot arena tours at Etihad and Coca-Cola Arena, stand-up specials at Dubai Opera, festivals, brand nights and weddings.

Artists get frames they actually want to post. Organisers get a same-night selection for socials and the full edited gallery inside 48 hours. Nobody gets asked to pose.
